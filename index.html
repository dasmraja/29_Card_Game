How to play 29

Teams. Four players in two teams; partners sit opposite each other. Play goes clockwise.

Cards. 32 cards (7 to Ace of each suit). In every suit the order from high to low is J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7. Only four ranks score: Jack 3, Nine 2, Ace 1, Ten 1, for 28 points in all.

Bidding. Everyone gets 4 cards. Starting left of the dealer, players bid how many points their team will win (16 to 28) or pass. Each bid must be higher than the last; a player who passes is out of the auction. If the first three pass, the dealer must bid 16.

Trump. The highest bidder secretly chooses the trump suit from a suit in their hand. Then everyone gets 4 more cards (8 each).

Play. The player left of the dealer leads. You must follow the suit led if you can. The highest card of the led suit wins, unless trumps are played.

  • If you cannot follow suit while trump is still hidden, you may ask for trump. It is revealed to all, and if you hold a trump you must play one.
  • Trumps only count once trump has been revealed. The bidder cannot lead a trump before it is revealed (unless they hold nothing else).
  • Pair: after trump is revealed, a player holding the King and Queen of trump can show them on their turn, once their team has won a trick. If the bidding team shows it, their target drops by 4 (not below 16); if the other team shows it, the target rises by 4 (not above 28).

Scoring. If the bidding team takes at least its target in card points it gains 1 game point; otherwise it loses 1. The first team to reach +6 wins, and a team that falls to −6 loses (±3 in a short game).
